I led development of the sudden cardiac arrest screening feature based on deceleration capacity of rate (DC), introduced on the HONOR Watch 5 Ultra.

The feature is part of HONOR's health-study platform and uses heart-rate deceleration related signals to support heart-health risk screening research.

Key technical components include:

- Deceleration capacity theory and traditional heart-rate variability features.
- Representation learning over PPG and ECG signals.
- Contrastive and predictive learning for physiological signal embeddings.
- Large-scale wearable data processing and validation.
