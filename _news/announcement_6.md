The HONOR Watch 5 Ultra was released with the sudden cardiac arrest screening feature based on heart-rate deceleration capacity, i.e. DC. This is also the first self-developed feature in the HONOR Health Study platform :sparkles:. As the lead developer, I am honored to declare that this is the industry's first smart wearable device to support sudden cardiac arrest screening research using heart rate deceleration force detection to assess the vagus nerve's ability to regulate heart rate, identify potential heart health risks, and guide users in managing their heart health.

Official feature page: [HONOR Watch 5 Ultra](https://www.honor.com/cn/wearables/honor-watch-5-ultra/).

This feature was implemented with millions of heart-health-related physiological signal records by combining deceleration-capacity-related theory and deep learning algorithms, including but not limited to `contrastive (predictive) learning` for photoplethysmography and electrocardiogram signals, and `representational learning` combined with traditional deceleration capacity techniques.
