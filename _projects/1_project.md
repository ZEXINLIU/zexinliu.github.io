I work on health-related algorithms for wearable devices, connecting physiological signal processing, representation learning, calibration, and deployment constraints into product-ready features.

The work spans noisy real-world signals such as PPG and ECG, population-level robustness, model calibration, and clinically meaningful feature definitions. My recent commercialization work includes sudden cardiac arrest screening and continuous non-invasive blood pressure monitoring.

Representative directions:

- Physiological signal modeling for wearable sensors.
- Multimodal representation learning and contrastive/predictive pretraining.
- Domain adaptation and calibration across broad user populations.
- Productization of research prototypes into stable device features.
