My Ph.D. research focused on scientific computing and uncertainty quantification, especially recurrence algorithms for orthogonal polynomials.

In the univariate setting, I studied stable computation of three-term recurrence coefficients for generalized orthogonal polynomial families. In the multivariate setting, I developed algorithms for recurrence matrices that enable stable evaluation of multivariate orthogonal polynomial bases.
